Conversation Favicons

To show pictures, this add-on needs to request them from external services. Nothing is sent until you allow it.

What is sent, and to whom:

  • Domain names of the people in your messages, calendar events and contacts (e.g. company.com) to Google (google.com/s2/favicons) and, as a fallback, DuckDuckGo (icons.duckduckgo.com), to download each domain's favicon.

Nothing else is sent: no message content, no names, no full email addresses, no usage data. Results are cached locally in Thunderbird.

Without permission, domains are shown as coloured initials; custom favicons you add still work. You can change this at any time in the add-on's options.
